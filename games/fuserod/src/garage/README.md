# Fuserod campaign reference

The supplied DOS `SR2_DATA` is 3,607 bytes with SHA-256 `8e3393ae7e2b54eb14431b12ef4b86197029abbf00b6046eecce6957e17d3644`. Reading the 22 little-endian, 20-byte records at offset 0x18C produced 22 distinct picture references, class values 0–8 and prices spanning 0–4,600. The 44 little-endian offsets at 0x6E2 each point to a terminated string in the 1,277-byte pool at 0x91A. These are direct checks on the supplied file. The public reimplementation describes the record layout, the 22 people, the composited car body overlays and the weekly classifieds. [File formats](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/FORMATS.md)

Fuserod has 22 original models, 15 classified listings per week, up to 16 owned cars, and one workshop per car. The catalogue defines each car's body, paint, engine power, tyres, gearing and asking price. The station uses tenths of a gallon, four gallons in a newly acquired car and a 17-gallon tank. Race entry reserves fuel and any cash stake once. Closing the page retains the open ticket, vehicle and installed parts. A lost title transfers the selected car. An extra title for an owned model or a full garage pays trade value. [Garage specification](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/port/spec/garage.md)

Wednesday Grudge Night takes a $30 entry fee. A completed strip run within 80 seconds records the driver's dial-in in Fuse log ticks. Four knockout heats follow. The confirmed race result decides each heat, including an opponent's breakout before the player finishes. The championship requires the rival ladder, six wins on each road, a Grudge Night win and the counted-road win check. It progresses from street drag to mountain road to aqueduct for the title. Losing against the champion resets the series and removes three mountain and two aqueduct wins, clamped at zero. These rules derive from the published race and flow specifications. The campaign tests exercise the transitions against scripted results. [Race specification](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/port/spec/race.md) · [Game flow](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/port/spec/game_flow.md)

Fuserod's five rivals, car and part designs, purchase prices, fixed purses, title trade values, repair costs, weekly rotation and per-event calendar advance are original balancing choices. The DOS game also had individual sellers, negotiation, opponent appearance and acceptance rolls, fine-grained time advancement, fuel-pump animations and save slots. These systems have simpler counterparts or remain outside this campaign. DOS campaign trace parity is unverified.
