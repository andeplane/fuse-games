# Road contact

Fuserod checks its road edges using the separate collision routines from the 1991 DOS build. On a clear canyon segment the tyres first enter the gravel, then the car falls off the verge. On the strip a right-hand wall scrape below 40 mph knocks off up to 10 mph and pushes the car left. At 40 mph or above the same impact retires the driver. The concrete aqueduct walls have hard limits on both sides.

The adapter maps Fuse's centreline and half-widths to original camera coordinates. Each canyon layout places the bridges, roadworks and right-drop guardrails at the distances produced by the DOS constructor. The renderer and contact detector read the same feature record. Trusses narrow the safe corridor. Entering rough pavement above 60 mph or striking a rock above 30 mph ends the race. The traffic generator places loose rocks from the road seed. The DOS game checks projected sprites. Fuse tests the seeded obstacle against both wheel tracks in road coordinates. The channel banks tilt the cockpit asymmetrically with the original roll angles. Each moving road frame on the concrete banks pushes the driver inward. Crossing the drainage trough kicks the tyres and costs two mph above ten mph. Native flag 2 interrupts the trough and suspends this penalty. The DOS renderer additionally deforms the channel floor and pitches the camera.

A faster driver closes on a leading rival within one 64-unit segment and 180 original lateral units. The DOS gate also tested two historical sprite poses to establish which driver was behind. Fuserod uses their relative positions on the preceding tick and sweeps the crossing for network play with more than two drivers.

The original front-view projection uses signed 16-bit camera offsets, a depth divisor and a 160-pixel horizontal centre. Its mirror has separate scale and origin. Fuse's renderer uses a responsive projection and original course art.

## Verification

The DOS race reset selects segments 8–88 for the sanctioned strip, 8–342 for the Aqueduct and 370–960 for Mulholland. A segment contains 64 motor position units. The aqueduct runs straight and level along its axis. Wall deformation and pitch depend on the vehicle's lateral position. Mulholland selects one of ten maneuver sequences. Its constructor builds signed heading ramps, banks, bridge decks and ten-segment elevation changes. The Fuse sampler implements the normal-speed PC setting (seven heading units per turning segment). The low-performance DOS setting uses six. The sampler clamps queries to the race endpoints and returns the current segment's geometric state. Fuse uses a world-space feature interval for contact. The original collision routine also inspects next-segment flags and the most recently projected near edges.

The geometry probe executes `0dcb:24d8` from the supplied image ten times, with a fixed layout argument and fresh track memory. It seeds the deterministic right-drop rail bits from the preceding road-setup pass. The TypeScript test checks every playable segment against hashes of the returned headings, heights, forward differences, bridge/works flags, bank heights and right-drop rails. This covers 5,910 canyon rows and 335 aqueduct flag words. The native dimensions and projection formula can be recovered from [race_phys.c](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/sr2port/src/game/race_phys.c#L215-L220), [race_track.c](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/sr2port/src/game/race_track.c#L229-L280) and [race_road.c](https://github.com/kylofon/street-rod-2-sdl3/blob/9a3d488143d108985422c2eeb9501e4aa5a0fe72/sr2port/src/game/race_road.c#L163-L193).

The oracle runs original x86 instructions at `0dcb:148a`, including the separate `0dcb:1cd2` aqueduct routine, the left/right bank functions at `0dcb:9d9c` and `0dcb:9e4e`, and the trough routine at `0dcb:9f08`. The image hash and unpacking procedure are shared with the [motor reference probe](../../tests/reference/README.md). The synthetic segment carries the requested flags, the tyres start undamaged, the driving speed and dashboard speed agree, and the sound vector returns immediately. Every case starts from fresh emulated RAM. Seven TypeScript tests compare 399 contact decisions on clear roads, 294 flagged canyon cases, 401 banking angles, and 729 channel camera and speed updates against the DOS oracle. They also exercise multi-car contact boundaries.

uv run --with unicorn==2.1.4 games/fuserod/src/course/probe.py /path/to/SR2-unpacked.exe
