# Fuse Liero

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Friends playing real-time worm combat inside Fuse, on individual browsers or a shared TV with phone controllers. The user's reference is OpenLieroX; they selected a full Fuse neon treatment.

## Capabilities and constraints

Destructible caves, ninja rope, weapons, bots, and Fuse's existing peer-to-peer rollback multiplayer. Following user playtest feedback, the world is larger than each player's viewport: personal cameras follow the player's worm; shared TVs retain the full-world overview. No always-running simulation service. Browser controls must work on keyboards and touch screens. Stop at a reviewed PR for human playtesting.

## Brand commitments

Fuse Liero, with the existing Fuse menus, room layout, portal and neon/pixel aesthetic. Original artwork and implementation; credit the reference without implying OpenLieroX compatibility.
