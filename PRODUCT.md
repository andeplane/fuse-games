# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends play Fuse Games alone or together, on individual devices or a shared TV with phone controllers. Approved developers use `/dev/` to understand usage, investigate play and turn evidence into development work.

## Product Purpose

Make responsive, consistent games easy to play together. Give developers a navigable account of which games and rounds were played, by which signed-in accounts or guest browsers, and in which sessions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The developer workspace is admin-only. Google identity and server-owned capability grants authorize access.
- A player session continues across games, solo/multiplayer and reopened tabs until 30 minutes of inactivity.
- Raw events, player sessions, reports, recordings and action proposals have distinct meanings. Historical metrics identify anonymous browsers, not verified people.
- Event dimensions are navigable filters. Multiple constraints combine with AND/OR. Selection determines available property columns.
- The assistant uses GPT-6.1 Sol and the same visible workspace controls as the human, with server-side limits.
- GitHub connects to the signed-in player; proposals can be reviewed and published as issues. Private recordings and transcripts remain private.
- Existing analytics opt-out, Do Not Track, local-development guard and scrubbing remain in force.

## Brand Commitments

Fuse Games retains its neon/pixel identity and shared UI tokens. The developer workspace prioritizes legible data and direct investigation.

## Product Principles

- Show meaningful activity before aggregate event volume.
- Make a displayed dimension a route to its underlying evidence.
- Distinguish complete results from bounded slices and unavailable history.
- Keep external writes reviewable and credentials on the server.
