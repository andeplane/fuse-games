# Lessons from merged Fuse Games PRs

The audit covered the 200 most recently merged PRs as of November 18, 2026, from #579 on October 25 through #807 on November 18. It used PR descriptions, changed-file lists, discussion, reviews and inline review threads. The examples below are documented defects and review findings, with related PRs included where they establish the intended behavior. They describe what happened at the time of the PR.

| What went wrong                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Check that would catch it                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The outer game-menu dialog stayed open after the game submenu closed, or focus stayed on the header trigger and consumed the next steering key. [#645](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/645), [#725](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/725), [#726](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/726)                                             | In a running match, close each menu layer by Back, Escape and Resume, then perform a gameplay action. Also open it with the keyboard and verify menu navigation works. |
| Auto-seat read the live join input during room updates and seated players under the first letter they typed. [#709](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/709), [#724](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/724)                                                                                                                                          | Compare a remembered name with a fresh name typed over several room updates. Leave a seat after automatic admission and check that the client stays unseated.          |
| Open and occupied seats had different heights. A stale seat on a rename form could also conflate renaming and moving. [#628](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/628), [#697](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/697)                                                                                                                                 | Add and remove a bot, move seats as host and guest, and try an occupied target. Assert stable geometry and seat identity when a move is refused.                       |
| A taller carousel buried Play in solo. Moving Speed into Options fixed solo while the separate room picker still showed the inline row. [#664](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/664), [#679](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/679)                                                                                                               | Open both real pickers on a short viewport. Verify Start, Options and picker actions in the right places and guest visibility in a separate context.                   |
| The fixed top bar covered page titles and game controls. In another composition its menu button extended past the desktop window. [#703](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/703), [#737](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/737), [#738](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/738), [#741](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/741) | Check the composed screen for overlap below the header and controls beyond the viewport, including phone landscape and a short desktop window.                         |
| A held shortcut ran through every speed step, the guest menu missed host changes, and restart could be submitted twice. [#686](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/686)                                                                                                                                                                                         | Hold the shortcut, keep a guest menu open across a host transition, and press Restart twice before the next room update.                                               |
| New songs started silently before audio finished downloading and decoding. The failure was easiest to reproduce with slow loading or a random selection. [#680](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/680), [#736](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/736)                                                                                              | Start cold and exercise failed download, retry, changed selection and the muted or controller-only paths. Separate download progress from playable readiness.          |
| Guides lacked actual controls, missed room copies, or used layouts and colors that made phone instructions hard to read. [#795](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/795), [#797](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/797)                                                                                                                              | Open live Help before and during play, check keyboard and phone bindings, scroll to the last rule, and return with Back and Escape.                                    |
| A signaling reconnect discarded the seat and rebuilt the peer links. Maximizing a FuseOS game replaced its iframe and lost the active room. [#625](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/625), [#700](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/700)                                                                                                           | Interrupt signaling and maximize/restore in the relevant host environment. Check session and seat continuity, then play another move.                                  |
| Local navigation dropped the mute flag, and a Pages base-path change left asset paths pointing at the old deployment. [#613](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/613), [#636](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/636), [#791](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/791)                                                                       | Follow actual buttons and share links from the built page. Check canonical and supported legacy URLs, flag handling and asset loads.                                   |
| Filtering unpublishable legacy history rows could hide older valid matches. Solo progress also needed separate handling from competitive publication. [#776](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/776)                                                                                                                                                           | Seed mixed history with valid records beyond the first page. Check load-more and compare the personal and competitive views after solo and multiplayer play.           |
| The shared menu migration left browser tests driving retired buttons. Fixing the test paths also uncovered real focus and modal bugs. [#635](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/635), [#645](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/645), [#729](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/729)                                                       | Run the consuming games’ real browser paths when changing shared navigation. Check the resulting behavior after the click.                                             |

Related patterns for current work are documented in [Menu patterns](../../../../packages/fuse-ui/README.md#menu-patterns). For example, [#802](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/802) established separate Player options, Radio and Room settings and exercised host/guest isolation. [#719](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-games/pull/719) preserved the selected song across a local sort.
