# Fuse consistency checklist

Before editing, identify the affected screens and the shared component that owns each interaction. Read “Menu patterns” in `packages/fuse-ui/README.md`. Use the nearest working game as a behavioral reference. Fusemania has separate solo and room pickers. Fuse Riders’ `src/render/self-locator.ts` and Fuseball’s `src/render/scene.ts` show two ways to identify a player at kickoff.

Run the relevant checks below on the composed screens before submitting. A change to the shared kit also needs a caller search across `games/` and a check in a representative consuming game.

| Surface                    | What to check in play                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Landing and menu           | Open the portal, How to Play and game menu through their real buttons. Use the shared top bar, dialog and Back controls. Every menu row visibly names its action. Check device audio in Player options and Radio where available. Exercise Back and Escape, return focus to the opener, and confirm gameplay input resumes after dismissal.                                                                                                               |
| Lobby and options          | Enter through solo, host and guest paths that the change affects. The primary start action stays by Back. Show the room code once. Host-owned settings synchronize to guests, local preferences remain per device, and each seat can tell what it may change. Where supported, try an empty seat, AI, one-player room and Shared TV.                                                                                                                      |
| Round start and identity   | At every new round, can each seated player point to their own piece before acting? Label it **YOU** on their own board, keep the label and pointer in bounds, and follow the piece if it starts moving. On a controller-only phone, identify its seat or truck. TVs, demos and spectators must never label the leader as their own. For games without a moving piece, mark the player's board or seat instead. Try two distinct seats and the next round. |
| Help, controls and HUD     | Teach the actual bindings and goal. Show applicable controls during each countdown without hiding the starting pieces. Check keyboard-only play, coarse-pointer targets, overlapping fingers, and cancellation when a dialog opens. Touch driving buttons should fit the phone screen. Desktop drivers should see their keyboard bindings. A key consumed by play must not scroll the page.                                                               |
| Art and legibility         | Check the real arena at gameplay scale. Match lobby previews, seat colors, piece identities and HUD colors. New pickups and effects should read against the track, use consistent perspective and lighting, and respect draw order when a player crosses them.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Shop and round transitions | If purchases exist, buy as a guest, verify ownership, balance and the next grid, and let the host start while the guest still has the shop open. A closing dialog must release input. Confirm next-round controls and identity are refreshed.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Results and replay         | Use the shared results screen. Show standings, who won, and a visible next action for the current role. Check a short desktop viewport and phone landscape for clipped actions or unnecessary scrolling. Play again or go back through the real menu.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Checks prompted by past fixes

Run these when changing the corresponding behavior. See [PR examples](references/pr-lessons.md) for the failures each check catches.

- **Phone gameplay controls.** Put touch buttons inside the game viewport as overlays, give the arena the available screen space, and keep the HUD readable. Play a real match in portrait and landscape and check that the full arena stays visible, every control fits in the viewport, and each target is at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels. A phone used solely as a shared-TV controller can use a dedicated controller screen.
- **Nested menus and input.** Try Back, Escape, and Resume from inside the nested menu during real play. Hold a gameplay key when opening it, release inside it, and move or act after closing it. Check that sliders, text fields and focused buttons get their native keys. An unfocused Enter activates the current selection once, even after clicking a different action.
- **Room entry and seat changes.** Test a fresh browser typing a name slowly during room updates and a browser with a remembered name. The first submits only on confirmation. The second auto-seats once where supported and can give up its seat. Try moving to a free seat and competing for the same seat. A rejected or stale request keeps the player's original seat, and Join/Add AI/Remove keeps the layout steady.
- **Host actions and local options.** Open the same picker in solo, host and guest flows. Confirm shared rules travel to the room, personal settings stay on the device, and top-bar shortcuts reflect their corresponding options. For long lists, scroll to the bottom and check that Start stays reachable beside Back and picker utilities stay with the selection. Sorting and filtering preserve a still-available selection by ID.
- **Game content readiness.** When changing how a game loads songs, maps or other required assets, start with a cold cache, switch selections during a load and retry a failure. Start a round once its content is usable. Where applicable, confirm that muted or controller-only devices skip assets they don't need. Test host restart or exit while a guest has a modal open when changing round transitions.
- **Invite and share links.** When changing routing, follow a real link from a fresh browser and check that it restores the intended room or selection. Preserve local preview flags in local navigation and keep them out of public share URLs.
- **Shared session continuity.** The shared room runtime owns reconnect and seat recovery. Run its recovery tests and check a representative game when changing that runtime or a game's room integration. FuseOS owns embedded window lifecycle. Check maximize and restore when changing the shell or its embedding integration.
- **Results and history.** Exercise the final result and next round or rematch, including a guest and a spectator where supported. Verify names, placement, and the role's available actions against the finished round. For history changes, check that a signed-in solo game keeps its personal history, competitive publication follows the platform rules, and older valid entries still load after filtering.
- **Browser verification.** Find and update the existing smoke and browser coverage flows for every menu or route changed. Drive visible controls through the real entry path. Assert the effect of an action, such as movement after Resume or a retained seat after reconnect. Treat stale locators and a confirmed product regression as separate findings. Use the built app for URL, asset and registration changes.

Use the repository’s muted headless preview and screenshot workflow. For layout changes inspect 1440 px desktop, 360 px portrait and phone landscape. For identity or authority changes use separate browser contexts for the two seats and TV. Add a regression at the level that could reproduce the failure. Record the roles, rounds and viewport sizes exercised, any fix from the check, and any untested device or path in the PR.
